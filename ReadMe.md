### Halim Malçok

Software engineer focused on **MLOps and LLMOps**. I build the infrastructure that takes machine learning models and LLM applications into production and keeps them reliable there.

I currently work on the on-premise AI platform at Özdilek, covering the model lifecycle, model serving and the LLMOps processes of a RAG system. In parallel, I am pursuing an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering at Bursa Uludağ University, where my research focuses on drift-resilient MLOps architectures.
